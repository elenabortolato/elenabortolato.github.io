About

I work at CUNEF University in Madrid as Assistant Professor. Before that, I was a post-doctoral researcher in Statistics at Universitat Pompeu Fabra and the Data Science Center of the Barcelona School of Economics.

I earned my PhD in 2024 under the supervision of prof. Laura Ventura, co-supervised by prof. Pierre E. Jacob (ESSEC) and prof. Robin J. Ryder (Imperial College London).

Research

My research interests include the following areas. Click a card to read more.

Bayesian & frequentist inference

Bayesian and frequentist inference, robustness, and model misspecification.

Computational statistics & MCMC

Computational statistics and Monte Carlo methods, with particular emphasis on Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms on manifolds.

Simulation-based inference & statistical evidence

Simulation-based (likelihood-free) inference, uncertainty quantification, and measures of statistical evidence (including p-values and confidence distributions), together with their interplay with probabilistic machine learning.

Latent variables & high-dimensional data

Latent variable models, factor analysis, and statistical methods for high-dimensional data.

Approximate & objective Bayes

Approximate inference and objective methods in Bayesian statistics.

Biomedicine & health sciences

Applications of statistical methodology to biomedicine and health sciences.
